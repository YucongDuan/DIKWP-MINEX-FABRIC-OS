# Governance

A capability provider retains authority over its node, data, license, price, and execution policy. A requester retains authority over purpose, data classification, constraints, payment ceiling, and revocation. The planner has no authority to loosen either side's contract.

Changes to hard gates, permission semantics, execution allowlists, financial adapters, or data-class rules require public review and a versioned successor release. Historical manifests and certificates must remain addressable.
